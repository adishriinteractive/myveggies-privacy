Privacy Policy

Game: My Veggies | Developer: Adishri Interactive

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Adishri Interactive ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting the privacy of players who enjoy My Veggies. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user data, safety, and compliance with global privacy regulations, including the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play Families Policies.

Summary: My Veggies does NOT collect, store, or sell any personal information. You can enjoy the full game without creating an account or sharing any personal data.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We believe in data minimization. When you play My Veggies, we do not require registration, login, or any personal disclosures. Specifically, we do NOT collect:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or physical addresses
  • Precise location or GPS data
  • Device contacts, photos, or files
  • Payment or financial details (all in-app transactions, if any, are securely processed directly by Google Play)

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The game saves your progress locally on your device storage (such as unlocked levels, star achievements, lives, and audio settings). This data remains strictly on your device and is never uploaded to any external servers operated by us.

3. Third-Party Services and Advertisements

To support ongoing development while keeping the game free to play, My Veggies may display family-friendly advertisements through vetted partner networks (such as Google AdMob). These services may collect technical device identifiers solely for operational ad delivery and fraud prevention.

All ad requests are configured with:

  • Child-Directed Treatment: Configured in accordance with COPPA requirements.
  • Max Ad Content Rating: Filtered strictly to General Audiences (G) to ensure age-appropriate, wholesome creative content.
  • Non-Personalized Ads: Behavioral interest profiling and cross-app tracking are disabled.

4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

My Veggies is built for players of all ages, including families and children. We comply fully with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe any personal data was inadvertently transmitted, please contact us immediately so we can promptly delete it.

5. Your Choices and Controls

You can reset your game progress at any time by clearing the app data in your Android device settings. You can also opt out of advertising identifiers via your device’s Google Play Services settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
